# procurement_captsone

Company / Department
        ↓
Business Need Arises
        ↓
Employee identifies requirement
        ↓
Employee estimates quantity and cost
        ↓
Employee provides business justification
        ↓
Employee logs into Procurement Platform
        ↓
Creates DRAFT Purchase Request
        ↓
Reviews Request
        ↓
Submits Request
        ↓
Manager Approval
        ↓
Finance Budget Validation
        ↓
Procurement
        ↓
Vendor
        ↓
Payment
        ↓
Delivery / Completion


MVP
--------------------------------
Login
  ↓
Employee creates request
  ↓
Employee submits request
  ↓
Manager approves/rejects
  ↓
Finance approves/rejects
  ↓
Procurement selects vendor
  ↓
Payment is simulated
  ↓
Request becomes COMPLETED
